My interests sit at the intersection of quantitative finance, time-series modelling, and neuromorphic computing — a combination that's led me from building SNN-based sensor placement algorithms to designing alpha-generation frameworks for portfolio management firms.

Projects

Project 01 · Published

SNN-Based Optimal Sensor Placement

A comparative evaluation of Spiking Neural Networks against ANN, Hill Climbing, PSO, and Genetic Algorithms for 2D optimal sensor placement. The SNN-QP solver formulated coverage as a combinatorial optimisation problem balancing spatial coverage against sensor overlap penalties. Published as a peer-reviewed paper.

PythonMATLAB SNNQP Solver PSO / GA

Key Result
SNN matched PSO accuracy with lower computational overhead on sparse grids
